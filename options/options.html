Scam Detector settings

All analysis runs locally in your browser. The only network requests are the optional lookups described below.

Thresholds

Domain age lookup

Sends the site's domain name (never the full URL) to rdap.org, which redirects to the registry's public RDAP service. Results are cached for 7 days. Freshly registered domains are one of the strongest scam indicators, so this is on by default.

Phishing reports feed

A list of about 50,000 community-reported, verified phishing addresses is downloaded from data.phishtank.com a few times a day (about 2 MB each time) and stored on this device as hashes. Pages are matched locally: the addresses you visit are never sent anywhere. Reports are community data and are not guaranteed to be accurate.

Google Safe Browsing (optional)

Off by default. When on, the full address of every page you open is sent to Google's Safe Browsing Lookup service using your own API key (create one in Google Cloud, enable the Safe Browsing API and restrict the key to this extension). Google's terms allow non-commercial use only. Results are cached for up to 30 minutes.

Google works to provide the most accurate and up-to-date information about unsafe web resources. However, Google cannot guarantee that its information is comprehensive and error-free: some risky sites may not be identified, and some safe sites may be identified in error. Warnings based on this data are marked "Advisory provided by Google".

Marketplace chat monitor

Off by default. When on, chat text on those sites is checked locally (nothing is sent anywhere) for schemes such as "I'll send a courier, click the link to receive the payment", "forward me the SMS code", "the payment is held until you pay the insurance", "send the deposit before the viewing" or overpayment refunds, in English, Portuguese, Spanish and French. A banner explains the scheme when one appears.

Trusted domains

Domains you have chosen to trust are never flagged.

    Extra brands to protect

    One per line: Brand name | keyword1, keyword2 | realdomain.com, other.com. Keywords are matched inside hostnames; real domains are never flagged. Useful for your bank, employer or local services.
